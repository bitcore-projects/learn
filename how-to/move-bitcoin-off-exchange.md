<h1 align="center">How to move bitcoin from an exchange to your own wallet</h1>

> **Short answer:** Copy a receive address from Bitcore Wallet, paste it into the exchange withdrawal form, and send a small test first.

---

## You'll see this if
- You keep coins on an exchange
- You fear account freezes
- You want self-custody

## Why it happens
Withdrawing gives you direct control of the keys; a test payment confirms the address works. For background, read the [self-custody](https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [Bitcore Wallet and Electrum: how to choose](./bitcore-wallet-vs-electrum.md).

## How to fix it
1. Open Bitcore Wallet and copy a receive address.
2. Paste it into the exchange's Bitcoin withdrawal.
3. Check the first and last characters.
4. Send a small test amount.
5. Send the rest after it confirms.

## If that didn't work
If the exchange rejects the address, check that you chose the Bitcoin network. You can also check [fees](https://bitcorewallet.org/fees) and [Bitcore Wallet and Sparrow: how to choose](./bitcore-wallet-vs-sparrow.md).

## Prevention
Always verify the pasted address to beat clipboard malware. The [download](https://bitcorewallet.org/download) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**How long does it take?**  
Usually minutes to an hour.

**Are there fees?**  
The exchange and network may charge.
