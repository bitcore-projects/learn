> **Short answer:** Bitcore Wallet is free to install; the only cost is the Bitcoin network fee paid to miners when you send.

---

## You'll see this if
- You wonder why sending costs money
- A fee looked higher than last week
- You want cheaper sending

## Why it happens
Fees depend on transaction size in bytes and on how busy the network is, not on the amount of bitcoin sent. For background, read the [Bitcore Wallet fees page](https://bitcorewallet.org/fees) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [How to spot a fake Bitcoin wallet app](./fake-bitcoin-wallet-apps.md).

## How to fix it
1. Check the fee estimate before sending.
2. Choose a slower fee when you are not in a rush.
3. Combine many small coins during quiet periods.
4. Use native SegWit addresses where available.
5. Review the total before confirming.

## If that didn't work
If a fee looks wrong, cancel before confirming and check network conditions. You can also check [stuck transactions](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/stuck-bitcoin-transaction) and [How to plan Bitcoin inheritance](./bitcoin-inheritance-plan.md).

## Prevention
Send larger, fewer payments rather than many tiny ones. The [FAQ](https://bitcorewallet.org/faq) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Does Bitcore Wallet charge a fee?**  
No, installation is free.

**Why does amount not matter?**  
Fees are by data size.
