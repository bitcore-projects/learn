> **Short answer:** A desktop wallet is a hot wallet for daily use; keep long-term savings with stronger offline protection.

---

## You'll see this if
- You are moving bitcoin off an exchange
- You hold more than you can afford to lose
- You are unsure what 'cold storage' means

## Why it happens
Hot wallets are online and convenient; cold storage keeps keys offline and away from malware. For background, read the [hot vs cold wallet guide](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/hot-vs-cold-wallet) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [How to plan Bitcoin inheritance](./bitcoin-inheritance-plan.md).

## How to fix it
1. Decide how much you spend regularly.
2. Keep that amount in Bitcore Wallet on your desktop.
3. Protect savings offline or with multisig.
4. Back up every recovery phrase on paper.
5. Review your setup every few months.

## If that didn't work
If you are unsure, start with small amounts and grow as you learn. You can also check [multisig in Bitcore Wallet](https://bitcorewallet.org/multisig) and [How to store bitcoin safely at home](./store-bitcoin-safely.md).

## Prevention
Never keep everything in one place. The [security overview](https://bitcorewallet.org/security) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Is a desktop wallet safe?**  
Safe enough for spending money on a clean computer.

**What about multisig?**  
It splits control across keys.
