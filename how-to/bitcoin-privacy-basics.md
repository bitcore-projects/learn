> **Short answer:** Use fresh addresses, avoid sharing balances and be careful linking your identity to addresses.

---

## You'll see this if
- You worry about being tracked
- Exchanges know your addresses
- You reuse one address

## Why it happens
Bitcoin is public; privacy comes from habits. For background, read the [security](https://bitcorewallet.org/security) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [Is Bitcore Wallet safe to use?](./is-bitcore-wallet-safe.md).

## How to fix it
1. Use a new receive address each time.
2. Avoid posting addresses publicly.
3. Separate spending and savings wallets.
4. Use watch-only on shared machines carefully.
5. Keep your computer private.

## If that didn't work
If an address is linked to you, start using new addresses from now on. You can also check [self-custody](https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody) and [How to download a Bitcoin desktop wallet safely](./download-bitcoin-desktop-wallet.md).

## Prevention
Think before sharing transaction details. The [FAQ](https://bitcorewallet.org/faq) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Is Bitcoin anonymous?**  
No, it is pseudonymous.

**Can governments track it?**  
Public data can be analysed.
