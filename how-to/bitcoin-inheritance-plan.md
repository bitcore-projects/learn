<h1 align="center">How to plan Bitcoin inheritance</h1>

> **Short answer:** Leave clear instructions and secure access, such as multisig, so family can recover your bitcoin without exposing it today.

---

## You'll see this if
- You hold bitcoin and have dependants
- Nobody else knows how your wallet works
- You worry about a single point of failure

## Why it happens
Without a plan, coins locked behind your recovery phrase can be lost forever. For background, read the [bitcoin inheritance guide](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/bitcoin-inheritance) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [How to receive bitcoin in Bitcore Wallet](./receive-bitcoin.md).

## How to fix it
1. List your wallets without writing the phrases in the list.
2. Choose a trusted method, such as multisig with a family member.
3. Store recovery material securely.
4. Write plain-language instructions.
5. Review the plan yearly.

## If that didn't work
If you cannot use multisig, split instructions and backups across trusted places. You can also check [multisig](https://bitcorewallet.org/multisig) and [How to send bitcoin safely](./send-bitcoin.md).

## Prevention
Update the plan whenever wallets change. The [self-custody](https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Should I give my phrase to a lawyer?**  
Discuss safer options like multisig first.

**Is a will enough?**  
A will alone does not give technical access.
