<h1 align="center">What to do if you lost your recovery phrase</h1>

> **Short answer:** If the wallet still opens, create a new wallet now, back up its phrase and move your coins.

---

## You'll see this if
- You cannot find your paper backup
- The wallet still opens
- You are worried about a failed computer

## Why it happens
While the wallet is accessible you can still move funds to a properly backed-up wallet. For background, read the [seed phrase guide](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/seed-phrase) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [How to verify the Bitcore Wallet SHA-256 checksum](./verify-sha256-checksum.md).

## How to fix it
1. Do not close or uninstall the wallet.
2. Create a new wallet and back up its phrase on paper.
3. Send all funds to the new wallet.
4. Confirm arrival.
5. Retire the old wallet.

## If that didn't work
If the wallet no longer opens and no phrase exists, recovery is usually impossible; beware of 'recovery services'. You can also check [restore a wallet](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/restore-bitcoin-wallet) and [How to install Bitcore Wallet on Windows](./install-bitcoin-wallet-windows.md).

## Prevention
Keep two backups in separate places. The [security](https://bitcorewallet.org/security) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Can support recover it?**  
No one can.

**Are recovery services real?**  
Most are scams.
